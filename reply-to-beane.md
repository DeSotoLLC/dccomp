# Reply to James Beane

James,

Short answer — citations to look up, not arguments to read.

**Framing.** I don't teach any of this is required for salvation or to "demonstrate" holiness. Salvation is by Messiah's finished work. The question I'm answering is whether these things were given by YHWH, kept by Yeshua, walked in by the apostles, and inherited by those grafted in.

**Your Col 2:20–23.** Paul attacks *"touch not, taste not, handle not"* — those words are not in Torah. He calls them *"commandments of men"* (Yeshua's phrase, Matt 15:9 / Isa 29:13). Then read Col 2:16–17: the believers *were* keeping festivals, new moons, and Sabbaths; Paul tells them not to be judged for it. He does not tell them to stop.

**Tzitzit:** Num 15:38–39 (the command — a memory aid for the commandments); Matt 9:20 and 14:36 (Yeshua wore them — *kraspedon* in the LXX is *tzitzit*); 1 Cor 11:1 (imitate Him).

**Shema on the doorposts:** Deut 6:8–9 and 11:18–20 — written for the household itself, not a sign to others.

**Feasts:** Lev 23:2 (*"the appointed times of YHWH"* — His calendar). 1 Cor 5:7–8 (Paul to a Gentile church in Corinth: *"let us keep the feast"*). Acts 18:21, 20:6, 20:16 (Paul keeping the feasts after the cross). Acts 2 (the Spirit poured out on Shavuot — the timing is the point). Zech 14:16–19 (all nations keeping Tabernacles in the age to come).

Look them up. The question isn't whether anyone is *required* to keep these. The question is whether they were given, kept, and inherited.

Shalom,
Sergio
